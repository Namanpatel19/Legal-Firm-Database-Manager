Title: Punishment for Murder

Whoever commits murder shall be punished with death, or imprisonment for life.

Title: Theft

Section 420

Title: Cheating and Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property

Whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces the person deceived to deliver any property.

Section 307

Title: Attempt to Murder

Whoever does any act with such intention or knowledge that if death is caused, would be guilty of murder.

Section 376

Title: Rape

Punishment for the offence of rape, with imprisonment of not less than 10 years.

Whoever enters into or upon property in the possession of another with intent to commit an offence.
